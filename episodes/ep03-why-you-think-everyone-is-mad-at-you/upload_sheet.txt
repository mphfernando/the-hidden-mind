TITLE:
Why You Always Think Someone Is Mad at You (Psychology)

DESCRIPTION:
You send a long, funny message. They reply: "ok." With a period. And suddenly your whole evening is gone. Why does your brain decide they're mad at you, and why is it almost always wrong?

In this animated video, Milo spirals over a two-letter text, and we look at what psychology research says about tone in text messages, rejection sensitivity and the "liking gap", plus three simple steps for the next time you think someone is mad at you.
