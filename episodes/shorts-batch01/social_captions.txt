TIKTOK:
Said "love you, bye" to your boss? There's a name for it 😳
Your boss ends the call and you say "Okay, love you, bye." Psychologists call it an action slip: some phrases run on autopilot, and when you're not paying attention the autopilot grabs the wheel. Not a secret feeling, just a habit in the wrong place. 😳 Send this to a friend who needs it.

===== S02_the_name.mp4 — Tue 1 Dec 10:30 PM =====
TIKTOK:
Why you forget their name 2 seconds after hearing it 🤯
"Hi, I'm Sarah." Two seconds later: gone. It's called the next-in-line effect: while you wait for your turn to speak, your brain is busy planning what to say, so the words right before it barely get stored. 🤯 Send this to a friend who needs it.

TIKTOK:
Why your recorded voice sounds so weird 🎙️
That is not what you sound like… but it is. When you talk, you hear yourself through the air AND through the bones of your skull, which makes your voice sound deeper to you. A recording only catches the air. 🎙️ Send this to a friend who needs it.
